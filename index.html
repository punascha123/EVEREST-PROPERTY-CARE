Frequently asked questions

Do you offer cleaning services across all of Auckland?

Yes. We service West Auckland, North Shore, Central Auckland, East Auckland and South Auckland, including Henderson, Massey, Albany, Takapuna, Manukau, Botany and Papakura.

How much does a bond clean cost in Auckland?

It depends on the size and condition of the property. Contact us for a free, no-obligation quote — WINZ quotes are available for eligible clients.

Are you insured and police-vetted?

Yes. Every team member is police-vetted and we carry full public liability insurance, so your property is always protected.

Can I book a one-off clean or only regular contracts?

Both. We offer one-off cleans, bond cleans, and regular weekly, fortnightly or monthly contracts for homeowners, landlords and property managers.
